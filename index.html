ABOUT SHRAY & CO

A dependable link between quality producers and commercial buyers.

Shray & Co Supplies Ltd is a UK-based supplier of surfactants and speciality chemical raw materials, serving customers in the United Kingdom and international markets.

Our role is straightforward: understand the buyer's technical and commercial requirements, source competitively, coordinate documentation, and support the transaction from enquiry through to shipment.

We focus on long-term business relationships, responsive communication and commercially practical supply solutions for regular and container-volume requirements.
