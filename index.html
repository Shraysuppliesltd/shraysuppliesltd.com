FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Helpful answers before you enquire

Do you supply customers outside the UK?

Yes. Shray & Co Supplies Ltd supports both UK and international enquiries, subject to commercial review, product availability and destination-market requirements.

What products do you supply?

Our core product range includes SLES 70%, CAPB 35%, CAPB 45%, LABSA 96%, Polysorbate 20, Polysorbate 60 and Polysorbate 80. We may also review sourcing opportunities for other chemical raw materials based on customer requirements.

Can you provide technical documentation?

Where applicable, supporting documentation may be available, including Technical Data Sheets (TDS), Safety Data Sheets (SDS) and Certificates of Analysis (COA). Documentation availability is confirmed at enquiry stage.

How can I request a quotation?

You can request a quotation using the enquiry form, by emailing us directly or by calling our team. Please include the product, quantity required and destination country where possible.

What packaging options are available?

Packaging options typically include drums, IBC containers and bulk quantities, subject to availability. Packaging is confirmed for each enquiry.

Which industries do you support?

We support customers operating in personal care, household cleaning, industrial cleaning, detergent manufacturing, contract manufacturing and private-label products.

Do you provide samples?

Sample availability depends on the product and commercial requirements. Please contact us to discuss your specific needs.

How quickly can I receive a quotation?

We aim to acknowledge new enquiries within one working day. A detailed quotation may take longer where availability, documentation or destination-market checks are required.

What are your lead times?

Lead times depend on the product, pack size, quantity, stock position and delivery destination. We confirm an estimated lead time in writing with each quotation, before an order is placed.

Can you help source products not listed on the website?

Yes. We may be able to assist with sourcing other chemical raw materials through our supply network. Contact us with your requirements for review.

Are your products suitable for all markets?

Product specifications, regulatory status, availability and suitability are assessed on a case-by-case basis and confirmed in writing before any order is accepted.

Where is Shray & Co Supplies Ltd based?

Shray & Co Supplies Ltd is a UK-based commercial supplier of surfactants and speciality chemical raw materials, supporting customers in the UK and international markets.

What is SLES 70% used for?

SLES 70% is commonly used in shampoos, body washes, hand soaps, dishwashing liquids and other liquid cleaning formulations.

What is CAPB used for?

CAPB is commonly used in personal-care and cleaning products, where it may support foam performance, mildness and viscosity characteristics.

What is the difference between CAPB 35% and CAPB 45%?

CAPB 45% has a higher active concentration than CAPB 35%, which may offer storage and transportation efficiencies depending on the application.

What is LABSA 96% used for?

LABSA 96% is a detergent intermediate commonly used in the manufacture of laundry detergents, cleaning liquids and industrial cleaning products.
